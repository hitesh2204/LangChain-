Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport played between two teams.

Each team usually consists of eleven players.

The main formats of cricket are Test cricket, One Day Internationals, and Twenty20.

In Test cricket, a match can last up to five days.

One Day International matches normally consist of fifty overs per side.

Twenty20 cricket consists of twenty overs per side.

A cricket match consists of batting, bowling, and fielding.

The batting team tries to score runs.

The bowling and fielding team tries to dismiss batsmen and restrict the number of runs.

The International Cricket Council (ICC) is the global governing body for cricket.

Popular cricket tournaments include the Cricket World Cup and the Indian Premier League.
