
Football is one of the most popular sports in the world.
It is played between two teams, with each team trying to score goals by putting the ball into the opponent's goal.

Lionel Messi is an Argentine footballer known for his exceptional dribbling, passing, and goal-scoring ability.
He has played for clubs including Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain, and Inter Miami.

Cristiano Ronaldo is a Portuguese footballer known for his goal-scoring ability, athleticism, and finishing.
He has played for clubs including Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, and Al Nassr.

The FIFA World Cup is the most prestigious international football tournament.
National teams from around the world compete in the tournament, which is held every four years.

Football players can have different positions such as goalkeeper, defender, midfielder, and forward.
Each position has different responsibilities and contributes to the team's overall strategy.
